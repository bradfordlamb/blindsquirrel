    Plan prices: Power to Choose (Public Utility Commission of Texas) snapshot for ZIP 77584, taken November 22, 2026. Prices change daily; confirm on the plan's EFL before you sign up.

    “Verified” plans were priced from their full EFL pricing. “Estimated” plans were priced from the EFL's 500, 1,000 and 2,000 kWh average prices, which is close for most plans but can be off by a few dollars a month because the EFL rounds those prices. Treat plans within about $60 a year of each other as tied, and let the cancel fee and fine print decide. Bill-credit plans are flagged.
